Method & sources

      

        Auto: temp, RH, RH recovery, wind/gusts, precip, CAPE, VPD (Open-Meteo); thunder prob, Haines, LAL, mixing height, transport wind (NWS gridpoints); Red Flag/Watch (NWS alerts).

        Nature of the numbers: weather rows are an Open-Meteo/NWS model consensus for the point — not the RAWS point forecast. Fuels rows come from the FEMS observation + forecast datasets as of your last refresh — close to, but not a live mirror of, the FEMS site: issuance timing, multi-station aggregation, and the daily worst-case reduction can each move the numbers a few points.

        Computed here: HDW (max hourly VPD×wind), Fosberg FFWI, ventilation index, GSI, 7-day KBDI projection, ERC via your verified NFDRS4 fuel-model-Y port — each derived inside every point×model sample before aggregation.

        NFDRS values pull per RAWS station from FEMS (API or CSV) and aggregate across the area — mean or max, your call. Hourly FEMS exports reduce to the daily worst case (index max / moisture min, any hour). SPC / NWCC / CPC products are linked from the Outlooks card.

        Composite = weighted mean severity (0–4) → Low / Moderate / High / Very High / Extreme. Days 2–4 and 5–7 banners lean toward their worst day (60% max, 40% mean). Overridden cells show an orange dot.
